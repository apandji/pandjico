About the station

Pandji.co is a station — not a portfolio feed. A place with instruments, located in St. Louis, where work becomes legible because you can feel the conditions it was made under.

Why a station

A portfolio asks you to assess finished objects and move on. It performs competence. A station observes, records, and transmits — continuously, from a specific latitude, hour, and temperature. That situatedness is the point.

I tend this one. The work here sits at the intersection of tactility, environmental sensing, health technology, and the politics of interfaces. The projects are readings; the site infrastructure is the same practice at a different scale: make silent systems perceptible.

Instruments you’ll meet

  • Sky — color from time, weather, and place (share yours or keep St. Louis)
  • Collections — curated views of work for different contexts
  • Transmissions — a broadcast log (specified; not live yet)

The practice

Aria makes air quality felt. Egress makes space navigable through touch. Color Scroller makes behavioral training visible. The common thread: a system was operating silently; now you can sense it.

I’m Andrew Pandji — creative technologist, artist, and experience design leader. Eight-plus years in health and care, from pre-seed product to national health-system scale. Right now: interfaces grounded in tactility and care.
